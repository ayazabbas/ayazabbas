Safety checks

  • No private keys, seed phrases, backend, storage, or secret handling.
  • Signing uses only the injected EIP-1193 wallet provider.
  • Receipt data must match the expected sender, target, amount, contracts, and withdrawal hash.
  • Proof data is built from public DBK and Ethereum RPC calls and checked against the L2OutputOracle before signing.
  • No initiate-withdrawal or direct ETH transfer path exists in this app.
